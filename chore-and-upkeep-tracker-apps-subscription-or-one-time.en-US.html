Chore and upkeep tracker apps: subscription, one-time unlock or paid upfront?

Apps that show how long it’s been since you last did something (changed the sheets, replaced the AC filter, called your parents) charge in three different ways: a subscription, a one-time unlock inside a free app, or a price you pay before you download. Here is how two popular ones charge on the US App Store, and when our one-time-unlock app Mochi DoneStamp is, and is not, the better fit.

Mochi DoneStamp is our app. We describe the other apps using only what their public App Store pages say, and we list our own weak spots too. Checked on November 27, 2026 on the US App Store. Prices, offers and ratings change; check the current listing before you buy. App Store pages show up to 10 purchase items, so a list may not be complete.

First, what are you tracking?

  • Quitting a habit. If you want a counter that shows how long since your last cigarette, Days Since is built around that: its description is all about breaking bad habits.
  • Chores and upkeep that come back. Changing the sheets, an oil change, watering plants. Last Time Tracker and Mochi DoneStamp both describe one-tap logging and a history of past entries.
  • Just a record of when. Any of the three can show how long it has been; the difference below is mostly how you pay.

Over a year

  • Days Since monthly and yearly items: a subscription keeps renewing each month or year until you cancel it. Its “Count Up Club - Lifetime”: you pay once.
  • Last Time Tracker: you pay once, up front, when you download it.
  • Mochi DoneStamp: free to start; Pro is one payment on the App Store.

So paying once is not unique to us: Last Time Tracker is paid once up front, and Days Since sells a lifetime option. On the US App Store, Days Since’s “Count Up Club - Lifetime” costs more than our one-time unlock, and Last Time Tracker’s download price is a little more than it. If you only want to count days for a short while, Days Since’s cheapest monthly item can cost less than a one-time unlock, as long as you remember to cancel it.

Where Mochi DoneStamp is a good fit

  • Start free, pay once if you need more. The free version gives you one active event with unlimited history. Lifetime Pro, bought once, unlocks unlimited events and the complete feature set.
  • Tap once when it’s done. DoneStamp records the exact time, with Undo. You can backdate, edit or delete any entry.

  • On iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. Interactive Home and Lock Screen widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, and on Apple Watch you can see the last time and log it from your wrist.
  • Your history, your data. Optional notes and photos, interval insights, complete backup and JSON export. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Where Mochi DoneStamp is not the right choice

  • The free version tracks one thing. One active event is enough to see if you like it, but if you want to track the sheets, the filter and the car, you’ll need Pro.
  • It’s made for chores and upkeep. If your goal is quitting a habit, an app built around that, like Days Since, will suit you better.
  • No iCloud sync is described. Our listing says your core data stays on your device and doesn’t describe syncing your history between iPhone and iPad. Last Time Tracker, for example, lists iCloud sync across your devices.
  • Very few ratings. Mochi DoneStamp has a single US rating, so you have much less to go on than with the apps above.
  • Separate stores, separate purchases. Buying on the App Store doesn’t unlock the Android app, and the Google Play price may differ from the App Store price.

A quick way to check any tracker app

  1. Before you set up your list, open the app’s App Store page. Check the price button and scroll to In-App Purchases. Words like “monthly” or “yearly” usually mean a subscription. Words like “lifetime” usually mean you pay once.
  2. Check how many things the free version lets you track.
  3. Read a few of the newest 1–3 star reviews, not just the average rating.
  4. If you subscribe, note the renewal date. You can cancel in your Apple ID’s Subscriptions settings.
