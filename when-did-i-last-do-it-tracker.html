“When did I last do it?” How to track the last time you did anything

When did you last change the sheets, water the fern, call your grandmother, back up your photos or replace the water filter? If the honest answer is “a while ago, I think”, the problem is not memory. These jobs have no fixed date, so to-do lists and calendars handle them badly. What works is the opposite approach: record when you actually did it, and let the next time follow from that.

Why a to-do list does not answer this question

A to-do list is good at “do this once”. A calendar is good at “do this on the 3rd of every month”. Most household and life jobs are neither:

  • You water the plant when it needs it, which is roughly weekly, not every Monday.
  • You changed the sheets four days late, so the next change should count from then, not from the date you planned.
  • Some things have no schedule at all, you just want to know: when did I last call Dad?

A recurring reminder that fires on fixed dates slowly drifts away from reality and gets ignored. A log of the last time keeps the real answer one glance away.

How to set it up in Mochi DoneStamp

  1. Add the event you wonder about most, for example “Changed sheets”.
  2. If you already know the last date, backdate it. You can backdate, edit or delete any occurrence, so the history starts accurate.
  3. Add a rhythm only if it helps. Rhythms can be flexible or exact, from minutes to years. The next due time always restarts from when you actually finished.
  4. Next time you do it, tap once. The exact time is saved to the history; Undo is there if you tapped by mistake.
  5. Put it where you will see it. Interactive Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets let you log without opening the app, and Siri and Shortcuts work too.
