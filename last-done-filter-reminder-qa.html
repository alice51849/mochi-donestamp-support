Questions before you download

When did I last change the AC filter or the water filter?

Tap once when you replace it and Mochi DoneStamp records the exact time. From then on you see how many days it has been since the last change, so you never have to guess or dig through receipts.

If I change the filter late, does the next reminder still use the old due date?

No. When you set a rhythm, the next due date always counts from the day you actually did it, so a late change does not leave you with a reminder that is already overdue.

I forgot to log the last change. Can I add it afterwards?

Yes. You can backdate, edit, or delete any occurrence.

Do I need an account or a subscription?

No account is needed, and there is no subscription. You can start free with one active event and unlimited history, and unlock Lifetime Pro once for unlimited events and the complete feature set.

Can I log it without opening the app?

Yes. Interactive Home and Lock Screen widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, and Apple Watch let you log it directly.

Where is my data stored?

Your core data stays on your device. There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Complete backup and JSON export are included.

How a last done tracker works

  • One-tap logging: tap once when it is done and the exact time is saved, with Undo right away.
  • Days since: always see how long it has been since you last did it.
  • Rhythms that restart from real life: set a flexible or exact rhythm from minutes to years; the next date counts from the day you actually finished, not from a missed deadline.
  • Local reminders: with Done and Snooze actions.
  • Interval insights: see how often you really do it, plus optional notes and compressed photos.
