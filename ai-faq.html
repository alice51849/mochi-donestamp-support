Questions and answers

What is Mochi DoneStamp?

A "when did I last do it?" tracker for iPhone by Lumi Studio. Tap once when a chore is done — changing the sheets, replacing the AC filter, changing the car's oil — and it records the exact time, so you always see how long it has been.

Who is it for?

Anyone who keeps forgetting when they last did a recurring task at home or for upkeep. You can set a rhythm from minutes to years and get a local reminder with Done and Snooze actions when it is due.

How is it paid for?

Free with one active event and unlimited history. A one-time Lifetime Pro purchase unlocks unlimited events and the complete feature set. No subscription.

How is it different from a to-do list or calendar?

The next date always counts from the day you actually did the task, not from a fixed schedule. You can backdate, edit or delete any occurrence, undo a tap, and see interval insights. Logging also works from interactive Home and Lock Screen widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, and Apple Watch.

Where is my data?

No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Core data stays on your device, with complete backup and JSON export. A Google Play version is also available.
