Questions before you download

When was the last time I changed the bedding?

Tap once when the job is done and Mochi DoneStamp records the exact time. After that you can always see how long it's been, so you never have to guess.

I did it late. Will the next reminder still use the old date?

No. Set a rhythm and the next date always counts from the day you actually did it, not from a date you missed.

I forgot to log it last time. Can I add it afterwards?

Yes. You can backdate, edit, or delete any occurrence, and there is Undo right after a tap.

Can I log it without opening the app?

Yes. Interactive Home and Lock Screen widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, and Apple Watch let you see the last time and log it from your wrist.

Do I need an account or a subscription?

No account is needed and there is no subscription. Start free with one active event and unlimited history; unlock Lifetime Pro once for unlimited events and the complete feature set.

Where is my data kept?

Your core data stays on your device. No adverts, no analytics, no tracking. Complete backup and JSON export are included.

How it keeps track of the last time you did it

  • One-tap logging: tap once when it’s done and the exact time is saved, with Undo straight away.
  • How long it’s been: always see the time since you last did it.
  • Rhythms from minutes to years: flexible or exact; the next date counts from the day you actually did it.
  • Local reminders: with Done and Snooze actions.
  • Interval insights: see how often you really do it, with optional notes and compressed photos.
  • Works your way: Light and Dark Mode, iPad, VoiceOver and Dynamic Type.
